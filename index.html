Blood Groups

There are eight main blood groups. Each blood group has different compatibility.

  • A Positive (A+)
  • A Negative (A-)
  • B Positive (B+)
  • B Negative (B-)
  • AB Positive (AB+)
  • AB Negative (AB-)
  • O Positive (O+)
  • O Negative (O-)

O negative blood is commonly known as the universal red blood cell donor type.

Donation Eligibility

A person should generally meet the following conditions:

  • Should be in good health.
  • Should be at least 18 years old.
  • Should meet the required weight criteria.
  • Should not have any condition that makes donation unsafe.
  • Should pass the medical screening before donation.

Final eligibility is decided by a qualified medical professional.

Blood Donation Guidelines

  • Eat a healthy meal before donating blood.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Carry a valid identity card.
  • Get enough sleep before donation.
  • Follow the instructions given by medical staff.
  • Take proper rest after donating blood.

Important Information

Blood donation is a safe process when performed by trained medical professionals.

Donated blood is tested before it is used for patients. Regular voluntary blood donation helps maintain an adequate blood supply.
